From the making of the world to the age we are living in

Two lines, read downward. Ice on the left is the science side. Gold on the right is the myth side. Tinted bands are the ice age itself: the long glacial cold, the Younger Dryas, then the warm Holocene. The first age is the story as this universe tells it, set inside that cold. Atlantis sits just after the Younger Dryas ends. From Abraham on, the years are the familiar traditional chronology, rounded on purpose. The myth line is the story. The science line is geology and other people's claims. A bracket is a reading, a report, or someone else's theory.

The Quaternary ice age began about 2.6 million years ago and has not ended. We are in a warm pause inside it, the Holocene, which starts about 11,700 years ago. Plato's Atlantis date, nine thousand years before Solon, is about 9600 BCE, just after the Younger Dryas ends. The glacial dates are geology. The years from Abraham to Christ are traditional, and several are disputed by centuries. Egypt's own history is left off this page, except the Hawara monument and the Exodus. Brackets are readings, reports, or someone else's theory. After the resurrection the page only marks touchpoints.
