From the making of the world to the age we are living in

One line, read downward. Tinted bands are the ice age itself: the long glacial cold, the peak of the ice, the Younger Dryas, then the warm Holocene. The first age is the story as this universe tells it, set inside that cold. Atlantis is not in the deep freeze. It sits just after the Younger Dryas ends. From Abraham on, the years are the familiar traditional chronology, rounded on purpose. The myth track is the story. The science track, opposite it, is geology and other people's claims. A bracket is not a second date.

The Quaternary ice age began about 2.6 million years ago and has not ended. We are in a warm pause inside it, the Holocene, which starts about 11,700 years ago. Plato's Atlantis date, nine thousand years before Solon, is about 9600 BCE, just after the Younger Dryas ends, not during the Last Glacial Maximum. The glacial dates are geology. The years from Abraham to Christ are traditional, and several are disputed by centuries. Egypt's own history is left off this page, except the Hawara monument and the Exodus. Creation is not given a calendar year. Brackets are readings, reports, or someone else's theory. After the resurrection the page only marks touchpoints.
