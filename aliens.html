Egyptian monument onlyAbout 1860 BCE

Hawara

Amenemhat III, 12th Dynasty, mudbrick pyramid. Kept here at about 1860 BCE. It is Egyptian. It is not 9600 BCE, and it is not a retelling of Egyptian history.

A separate claim says the maze is still there, underground and intact, and that an alien saucer sits in the middle of it. That claim is an alternative reading. It is not what Petrie published, and it is not what the 2010 survey published.

The publication that sets the claim out, and that shows the pictures it presents as the scans, is Louis de Cordier's newsletter "The Merlin Burrows scans II." The pictures on that page are copyrighted. They are not copied here. Nothing on this page is a scan.

Do not confuse that claim with a different 2025 paper. Mark J. Carlotto, in the Journal of Scientific Exploration, argues from space-based radar for a possible underground structure at Hawara, which he suggests may be part of the labyrinth. That paper does not identify a saucer, and it is not a report of an excavated machine. It is linked so the two readings stay separate. It is not established archaeology either.
