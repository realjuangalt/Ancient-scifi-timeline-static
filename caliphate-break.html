750 CE

The break in the caliphate

In 750, at the Battle of the Great Zab, Marwan II was defeated. That defeat ends the unified Umayyad caliphate. Members of the Umayyad house were hunted down and killed, but one survivor, Abd al-Rahman, established himself as a ruler in Spain in 756, founding the Umayyads of Cordoba. The Abbasids take the east. An Umayyad line continues in al-Andalus.

This break is the hinge of the arc, not the whole of Islamic history, so later episodes can be added.
